# Will the Customer Accept the Coupon?

## Project Summary

This project analyzes 12,684 driving scenarios to identify factors that influence whether a customer accepts a mobile coupon while traveling. The goal is to understand the characteristics and behaviors associated with coupon acceptance.

## Main Findings

- Overall, 56.8% of coupons were accepted, while 43.2% were declined.
- Bar coupons had a lower acceptance rate (41.0%) than the overall average.
- Customers who already visited bars frequently were much more likely to accept bar coupons (76.9%) than infrequent bar visitors (37.1%).
- Drivers over age 25 who visited bars regularly accepted coupons at a significantly higher rate (69.5%) than other drivers (33.5%).
- Coffee House coupon acceptance increased dramatically among customers who already visited coffee houses regularly (65-69%) compared to those who never visited (18.9%).
- 
## Conclusion

The strongest predictor of coupon acceptance was prior customer behavior. Customers were more likely to accept a coupon when it matched places they already visited, such as bars or coffee houses. These findings suggest that targeted, interest-based promotions can be more effective than broad marketing campaigns.
These findings describe patterns in the survey data. They show relationships between customer characteristics and coupon acceptance, but they do not prove that any one characteristic directly caused a customer to accept a coupon.
